# 2 Player Battle
Console-based two-player battle. One turn per player.Types of players are Powerful, skillful, raged. Different stats for health, heal, damage.Attack or heal per turn. 20% chance for special ability. Players alternate turns attacking or healing. Ends when one player's health hits zero.
